nas-rclone

nas-rclone 是个人自用的 NAS 备份工具，通过 rclone 把家用 NAS 上的文件备份到本人自己的 Google 云端硬盘。

nas-rclone is a personal backup tool. It uses rclone to back up files from a home NAS to the owner's own Google Drive.
